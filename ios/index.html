Privacy Policy

Privacy information for LEEBI on iPhone and iPad.

Effective and last updated: October 27, 2026

English한국어Español

1. Audience and operator

LEEBI is a calendar, notes and personal finance app intended for adults aged 18 and over. Operator: HY3D Co., Ltd. Contact and privacy requests: seenell25@gmail.com.

2. Your records and on-device processing

Events, notes, photos, optional locations, finances, accounts, goals and contacts are stored on your device. Business-card OCR and analysis of pasted payment messages run on the device. These records are not sent to a developer server. Converting locations to addresses may use services provided by your operating system.

3. Optional permissions and app lock

Camera and photo access support attachments and card scanning; location access adds a location when you choose; notifications support reminders. Denying a permission leaves other features available. A hash of your app PIN is stored in iOS Keychain. Face ID is handled by the operating system; the app does not receive your biometric data.

4. Ads and privacy choices

The free version may show banner ads and app-open ads during startup. Google AdMob may process the advertising identifier (IDFA, when permitted), IP address, device information, interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. Google and advertising partners may process this information on overseas servers under their policies. Your record contents are not provided for ads. Where required, a UMP consent message is shown. Review, change or withdraw choices in More → Ad privacy settings. Manage tracking permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads start only when ad requests are allowed. Subscribers are not shown ads. See the Google policies below for retention, deletion and other processing details.

5. Apple subscriptions

Apple App Store handles Premium payments, renewals, cancellation and refunds. The app does not receive card numbers. StoreKit product and transaction identifiers, active status and expiry dates are stored on your device to determine access. Payment currency is independent of your currency book.

6. Backup, sharing and feedback

JSON backups contain all currency books and exclude photo files and your PIN. Files, cloud services and recipients you select follow their own policies. Send feedback opens an email draft containing app version and device information. When you send it, your email address, message and attachments are used to handle your inquiry. Remove sensitive records before sending. PC connections and Pro features are not available on iOS.

7. Retention, deletion and changes

You can edit or delete records, or remove the app. Keychain lock settings may remain after reinstalling. Exported files and operating-system backups must be managed separately. Request deletion and other rights for external-service data through that service or the operator contact. Changes to processing will be reflected in this notice and the published policy.
